    間 · ma

    Why “Ma”

    Ma (間) is the Japanese sense of an interval with a shape: the pause that turns two notes into a phrase, the margin that lets a page breathe. This journal tries to leave room in the same way. Few posts, long lines of thought, nothing that refreshes.
